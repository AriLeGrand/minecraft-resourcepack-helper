Resource packs,

Blockwright is a desktop app for making Minecraft Java Edition resource packs. It finds the game on your computer, shows every texture of the version you pick, and gives you a pixel editor, animation, 3D previews and an export that is ready to play. No Aseprite or other program needed.

The tour

One app, every step of a pack

From the first texture to the file your friends install. Click a screenshot to see it full size.

Every texture of your game version

Blocks, items, GUI, entities, particles: all of them, by category, with search and filters. The inspector shows the game's texture next to yours, the block models that use it and its name in older and newer versions.

  • Status at a glance: to do, done, changed since export
  • What's new since… lists new, redrawn, renamed and removed textures between two versions
  • Batch import a folder of PNGs, matched to their textures, old names translated
  • Share list as a checklist for Discord, GitHub or Notion, text or a spreadsheet
  • Notes, tags and who does what; a History that restores any earlier version
  • A Progress card: percent done overall and per category

A pixel editor in the style of Aseprite

Pencil, eraser, bucket, line, shapes, selections, move, gradient, text, symmetry and tiled mode, with layers and undo. A faint vanilla reference layer sits under your drawing if you want it.

  • Preview tiled, as it is, animated, or on a 3D block that updates while you draw
  • A pack palette shared by every texture, with "Snap to pack palette"
  • Seam check in the tiled preview when a block's edges show
  • Recolour a whole set of textures; make variants of a family (oak → spruce, birch…)
  • Copy and paste with other programs through the Windows clipboard

Animated textures without hand-written JSON

Frames with their own durations, onion skin and playback at the game's speed. Blockwright writes the .mcmeta file for you and shows it.

  • Smooth frames ("interpolate") and a custom frame order
  • Import a vertical strip of frames
  • Watch it play on a 3D block with day, night and light level controls

Redraw the game's screens in place

The pause menu, inventory, HUD, title screen, recipe book, advancements and boss bars, put together from your pack's pieces at GUI scale 1×, 2× or 3×.

  • Click a piece to select it, double-click to draw on it
  • Choose how it stretches: Stretch, Tile or Nine-slice with borders
  • Versions before 1.20.2 keep pieces in shared images: you draw only inside each piece's rectangle

Particles, moving, in a little scene

All particles of the version in a small live scene: night, day or under water, from ¼× to 2× speed, with your pack's textures or the game's.

  • For old versions with one particle sheet, see which cells belong to a particle
  • Draw only on those cells, nothing else gets touched

Checked, exported, installed

Pack icon, name and a description with § colour codes. Checks with one-click fixes, then export as a .zip or a folder.

  • Install straight into Minecraft's resourcepacks folder
  • Copies for older versions, files renamed automatically
  • Smaller PNG files with exactly the same pixels
  • A 1920×1080 showcase image and page text for CurseForge or Modrinth
  • Compare with another pack, side by side, and copy what you tick

More in the box

Small tools that save whole evenings

  • Scene, in 3D

    A small diorama with your textures: a house, a 4×4 wall to check seams, a garden, or your own scene up to 8×8×4. Day, sunset or night, block light 0–15 and ambient occlusion, like in the game.

  • Variety

    Random variants with weights, built into the game, no mod. Connected textures for OptiFine or Continuity: the 5 tiles and the .properties file, with a live preview.

  • Pack palette

    One palette for the whole pack. Snap every drawn colour to it, and get an export check for colours that slipped outside.

  • Recolour and variants

    Shift hue, saturation, brightness or contrast for a set of textures at once, with before/after previews and Undo. Turn oak planks into spruce, birch, cherry…

  • Made for teams

    Notes, tags like "needs redo", and who does each texture. Filter by person, share the to-do list grouped by name, restore any earlier version from History.

  • Higher resolutions

    Packs from 16× to 256×. A smooth Scale2x or Scale3x start for 32× packs and up, so you are not redrawing from a blur.

How it works

Three steps to a pack you can play

  1. 1

    Pick your game version

    Blockwright finds Minecraft on your computer and lists the versions you have installed. Choose one, a resolution and a folder. The textures are read from your own game.

  2. 2

    Draw or import

    Double-click a texture to redraw it, or bring in a whole folder of PNGs at once. Press Ctrl+S and it is in your pack.

  3. 3

    Export and install

    Make the checks green, click "Export pack", and tick "Install into Minecraft's resourcepacks folder". Then turn it on in Options → Resource Packs.
