Features

Explore what Blockwright can do.

Every texture of your game version.

  • What's new since… lists new, redrawn, renamed and removed textures between two versions
  • Batch import a folder of PNGs, matched to their textures, old names translated
  • Share list as a checklist for Discord, GitHub or Notion
  • Notes, tags and who does what; a History that restores any earlier version

A pixel editor in the style of Aseprite.

  • Pencil, bucket, shapes, selections, gradient, text, symmetry and tiled mode, with layers and undo
  • A pack palette shared by every texture, with "Snap to pack palette"
  • Seam check when a block's edges show in the tiled preview
  • Copy and paste with other programs through the Windows clipboard

Animated textures, played at the game's speed.

  • Frames with their own durations, onion skin and smooth frames ("interpolate")
  • Import a vertical strip of frames; custom frame order
  • Watch it on a 3D block with day, night and light level controls

The game's screens, rebuilt from your pieces.

  • Pause menu, inventory, HUD, title screen, recipe book, advancements and boss bars at GUI scale 1×, 2× or 3×
  • Stretch, Tile or Nine-slice with borders
  • Old versions: draw only inside each piece's rectangle of the shared image

Particles, moving, in a little scene.

  • Night, day or under water, from ¼× to 2× speed
  • Your pack's textures or the game's, side by side
  • Old single sheets: see and draw only the cells a particle uses

Checked, exported, installed.

  • Install straight into Minecraft's resourcepacks folder
  • Smaller PNG files with exactly the same pixels
  • A 1920×1080 showcase image and page text for CurseForge or Modrinth
  • Compare with another pack and copy what you tick

More in the box

Small tools that save whole evenings.

  • Scene, in 3D

    A house, a 4×4 wall to check seams, a garden, or your own scene up to 8×8×4. Day, sunset or night, block light 0–15 and ambient occlusion.

  • Variety

    Random variants with weights, built into the game. Connected textures for OptiFine or Continuity, with a live preview.

  • Pack palette

    One palette for the whole pack. Snap every drawn colour to it, and get a warning for colours that slipped outside.

  • Recolour and variants

    Shift a whole set of textures at once with previews and Undo. Turn oak planks into spruce, birch, cherry…

  • Made for teams

    Notes, tags and who does each texture. Share the to-do list grouped by name; restore any earlier version.

  • Higher resolutions

    Packs from 16× to 256×, with a smooth Scale2x or Scale3x start so you are not redrawing from a blur.

How it works

Three steps to a pack you can play.

  1. 1

    Pick your game version

    Blockwright finds Minecraft on your computer. Choose a version, a resolution and a folder.

  2. 2

    Draw or import

    Double-click a texture to redraw it, or bring in a folder of PNGs. Ctrl+S and it is in your pack.

  3. 3

    Export and install

    Make the checks green, export, and turn it on in Options → Resource Packs.
